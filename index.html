Programs We Offer

XI & XII Commerce (CBSE)

As an educational institute, we can provide concept-based learning with practical exposure, helping CBSE Commerce students understand subjects deeply, not just memorize. Along with this, we offer career guidance and skill-oriented training to prepare them for CA, CMA, CS, and future professional success.

  • ✓ Economics
  • ✓ Business Studies
  • ✓ Mathematics

XI & XII Commerce (GSEB)

  • ✓ Economics
  • ✓ Organization of Commmerce & Management
  • ✓ English
